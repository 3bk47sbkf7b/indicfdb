The benchmark

Conversation is more than answering.

IndicFDB extends full-duplex evaluation to Indian English, Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, and Malayalam. Every language has 1,235 samples spanning the same four behaviors.

How it works

Built for multilingual speech.

Natural pause, turn-taking, and backchannel samples are mined from channel-separated conversations. User interruption samples are constructed and validated across languages. A VAD-based evaluation pipeline measures conversational timing without requiring word-level alignment.

  1. Mine conversational events with multi-threshold voice activity detection and human review.
  2. Stream the input to a voice agent and capture its response audio.
  3. Evaluate timing with language-independent heuristics, then transcribe, translate, and rate interruption responses with open-weight models.
